## Recommended sequence (your first 14 days)

### Day 1 — Setup
1. **Morning:** Work through `05-deployment-guide.md` → live at `app.manaraos.ae` by EOD
2. **Afternoon:** Convert `01-pitch-deck.md` to actual slides (use Gamma — 60 seconds)
3. **Evening:** Read `02-demo-script.md` cover-to-cover, do one solo run-through

### Day 2 — Outreach prep
1. Buy Sales Navigator trial ($99 first month)
2. Build prospect list in CRM template (`04-prospect-list.md` bottom)
3. Verify decision-makers for top 20 Tier 2 companies
4. Personalise the LinkedIn DM template with each company's unit count

### Days 3-5 — Outreach burst
1. 10 LinkedIn DMs per day (only Tier 2 first)
2. 10 cold emails per day (use Hunter.io for emails)
3. WhatsApp the 5 Tier 3 founders directly
4. Total: 70 cold touches in 3 days

### Days 6-10 — Demos
1. Whoever responds → book demo within 48 hours of their reply
2. Use `02-demo-script.md` to the letter
3. Send follow-up email from `03-followup-emails.md` within 30 minutes
4. Aim for 5 demos done by Day 10

### Days 11-14 — Close
1. 2nd touch on demos that haven't responded
2. 3rd touch on the "let me think" prospects
3. Pilot agreements sent to anyone who said yes
4. **Target: 1 signed pilot by Day 14**

## Common founder mistakes to avoid

1. **"Just one more feature before I sell"** — No. Sell what exists.
2. **Mass-emailing all 50 prospects with the same template** — Personalise the first 20.
3. **Following up only by email** — Voice notes on WhatsApp win UAE deals.
4. **Discounting on first ask** — Hold the line. Pilot is free; that IS the discount.
5. **Demo > 15 min** — They tune out at minute 16. Trim ruthlessly.
6. **Promising features that don't exist** — Will burn your credibility for years.
7. **Building before you have customer commitments** — Get LOIs first, then build their requests.
