## SLIDE 2 — The pain

### Title
**Property managers in the UAE are running an enterprise on Excel and WhatsApp.**

### Body (3 bullets, each one a punch in the gut)

**1. Compliance roulette**
You're juggling trade licence renewal, quarterly VAT, RERA registration, civil defence cert, fire safety AMC, every staff member's EID + visa, building insurance — across multiple properties. Miss one date and you're paying AED 10,000 in fines per item.

**2. Owner trust is your single point of failure**
Owners ask: "where did my AED 850 in maintenance charges go?" You scramble through emails to find vendor invoices. One bad statement → owner switches to another PM. **The average UAE PM loses 18% of owners per year to trust issues.**

**3. Vendor kickbacks are eating your margin**
Plumber quotes AED 1,200 for a sink leak. Your in-house team takes it. Six months later you discover the going rate was AED 600. Multiply by hundreds of tickets per year.

### Visual
- Three icons: 📋 (compliance) · 💔 (owner trust) · 🔧 (vendors)
- Statistic callout: "**AED 1.2M** — average lost annual revenue per 100-unit PM from compliance fines, owner churn, and vendor over-charging combined"

### Speaker notes (45 seconds)
"How many of these resonate? [pause] Right. We built Manara OS because we've watched UAE PMs lose hundreds of thousands of dirhams to problems that are entirely solvable with software. Let me show you the next 13 minutes how."

## SLIDE 3 — What Manara OS gives you

### Title
**Five capabilities your current toolset does not have. Period.**

### Body — 5 differentiators in a 2-column grid

**1. UAE Compliance Calendar**
Every trade licence, VAT return, RERA, DLD, civil defence cert, staff visa — tracked in one place. Auto-reminders at 60/30/7 days. WhatsApp + email. Never pay another fine.

**2. Receipt Vault for Owners**
Every vendor invoice attached to the right property with before/after photos. FTA-tax-ready export. Owners can approve any pending charge from their phone. **Owner churn drops to single digits.**

**3. Vendor Bid Marketplace + Leaderboard**
Open a maintenance ticket → up to 8 approved vendors auto-bid → AI ranks them by price, ETA, warranty, rework rate. Average savings: **22% per ticket vs your current panel.**

**4. AECB Credit Reporting**
On-time-paying tenants opt in. We report monthly to UAE's Al Etihad Credit Bureau. **Tenants stay an extra 14 months on average — because leaving means losing their credit score progress.**

**5. AI everywhere it matters**
Tenant screening (AECB + WPS + Etisalat + AML in 90 seconds). AI calls in EN/AR. AI-drafted Google review responses. Per-persona AI reports for you, owners, tenants, vendors.

### Visual
- Two-column grid of 5 features, each with an icon
- Bottom strip: "+ Multi-language (Arabic RTL, Hindi, Urdu, Tagalog) · 5 native mobile apps · Free pilot for first 90 days"

## Design conventions

- **Brand colours:** Amber #D97706 (primary), Slate #0F172A (text), White
- **Typography:** Inter / SF Pro for English, Noto Kufi Arabic for Arabic
- **No emojis on slides 4-5** — keep these formal-looking. Slides 1-3 can be playful.
- **Slide 2 should make them uncomfortable.** That's the point. If they're nodding along, you're winning.
- **Slide 5 is always 3 options, never 2 or 4.** Three is the magic number for decision psychology.
