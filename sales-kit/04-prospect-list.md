## How to find verified decision-makers

### LinkedIn Sales Navigator (recommended, $99/mo first month)
1. Filter: Title contains "Property Manager" OR "Head of Property" OR "Director of Property"
2. Geography: United Arab Emirates
3. Company: paste in 5-10 names from list above
4. Save 50 leads
5. Send 20 connection requests/day with a personal note

### LinkedIn free (slower but works)
1. Search the company name + "property"
2. Look for 2nd-degree connections — ask a mutual contact for intro
3. If no mutual: send connection request with this note (300-char limit):

> "Hi [name], building Manara OS — the first UAE-native PM platform with AECB credit reporting + FTA tax certs. Saw [their company] manages [X] units in [area]. Would love 15 min to show you. No sales, just whether it's useful."

### Hunter.io / Apollo.io / RocketReach
- $50-99/mo for verified email addresses
- Search by company name → returns role-based emails
- Useful for the "send a Loom video to their inbox" approach

### Cold WhatsApp (UAE specific — works better than email)
1. Find their personal mobile (often listed publicly on Bayut listings they manage, or via a quick Google)
2. Send a polite voice note in English (or Arabic if comfortable)
3. Voice notes get 70%+ response rate in UAE B2B vs 15% for text

## The "10x10x10" outreach playbook

**Goal:** From this list, book 3 demos in 14 days.

### Week 1
- **Day 1-2:** Send 10 LinkedIn connection requests (Tier 2 first). Personal note required.
- **Day 3-4:** Send 10 cold emails to verified emails (use Hunter.io). Subject: see template.
- **Day 5:** WhatsApp 10 founders directly (Tier 3 first — they answer phones).
- **Weekend:** Look at responses; follow up on opens.

### Week 2
- **Day 1:** Send 10 personalised Loom videos to non-responders. 60 seconds each. Show them their company's name in the demo workspace.
- **Day 3:** Re-engage anyone who opened the email but didn't reply.
- **Day 5:** Phone call any "warm" prospects (opened, replied, but didn't book).

**Expected outcome:** From 30 attempts → 6-10 responses → 3-5 demos booked → 1-2 paying customers.

## Outreach scripts

### LinkedIn DM (300 chars max)

```
Hi [first name] — building Manara OS, a UAE-native PM platform with AECB credit reporting, FTA tax certs, vendor bid marketplace. Saw [company] manages [X] units. 15-min demo this week? Free 90-day pilot for first 5 customers. Calendar: [link]
```

### Cold email — Subject + body

**Subject:** `[Their first name], unique to UAE PMs`

**Body:**

```
Hi [first name],

Quick question — does your current PM software let your tenants build their AECB credit score from rent payments?

Mine does. It's the reason I'm building Manara OS — the first PM platform designed FOR the UAE, not adapted to it.

Three things it does that nothing else does:

1. AECB credit reporting for opt-in tenants (we're a member)
2. FTA-compliant annual tax certificates for owners
3. Vendor bid marketplace with AI ranking — averaging 22% savings

15 minutes next week? Free 90-day pilot for first 5 customers this quarter.

Calendar: [your Calendly]

[Your name]
Founder, Manara OS
```

### WhatsApp voice note (60 seconds — UAE-specific killer)

```
Hi [first name], this is [your name] from Manara OS.

I noticed you run [Company] and manage about [X] units in [area].

I'm a [your background] and I've built what I think is the first UAE-native property management platform. We do things nothing else does — AECB credit reporting for tenants, FTA tax certs for owners, an AI-ranked vendor bid marketplace.

I'd love just 15 minutes to show it to you. I'm offering a free 90-day pilot for the first 5 PMs I onboard this quarter, no card required.

If now's not a good time, no worries — but if you can spare a quarter hour, my calendar's at [shortened link].

Either way, all the best with [Company]. Have a good one.
```

**Why voice over text:** In UAE business culture voice notes signal respect, time investment, and seriousness. Open rates ~95% vs ~30% for cold text.

## What to do if 50 isn't enough

After working this list:

### Sources for more
- **RERA Broker Registry** — https://dubailand.gov.ae (English + Arabic, public)
- **Bayut "Managed By"** filter on listings — gives you every active PM
- **Property Finder agency directory** — paywalled but worth it
- **LinkedIn company search** — filter "real estate" industry + "11-50" or "51-200" employees + UAE
- **DLD trustee registry** — for property managers, not brokers
- **UAE Yellow Pages** — old-school but still works

### Sources to avoid
- Generic UAE business databases — most data is stale or fabricated
- "Buy email lists" services — high spam rates, low quality
- Mass-blast tools like Reply.io for first 50 — relationships matter

## Honest expectations

From 50 cold prospects approached with this playbook:
- 12-18 will respond at all
- 6-10 will agree to a demo
- 4-7 will show up to the demo
- 2-4 will say "interesting, send pilot"
- 1-2 will actually start the pilot
- 0-1 will become paying customers in 90 days

**That's normal.** The trick is volume + speed. First 5 customers come from this list. Customers 5-50 come from referrals.
