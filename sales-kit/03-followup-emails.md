# Follow-up Email Templates

**Always send within 30 minutes of the demo ending.** Speed signals priority.

---

## Template 1 — Same-day thank-you (positive reception)

**Subject line:** `[Their first name], the 3 numbers from our session`

**Pre-header:** `Recap + what I promised to send + your pilot start date`

---

Thanks for your time today. Three numbers from our conversation that stuck with me:

1. **[N] units** under your management — comfortably in our Professional tier range.
2. **AED [X]** in vendor spend per year — the bid marketplace alone projects to save you AED [X × 0.22] in year one.
3. **[N] owner relationships** at risk of churn — the Receipt Vault is built precisely for that pain.

As promised:

- 📎 **Manara OS one-pager** [attach `04-onepager.pdf` once you build it]
- 🔗 **Calendar link for the 90-day pilot kickoff:** [your Calendly URL]
- 📹 **5-minute recording of the bid marketplace deep-dive:** [Loom/YouTube link]

The pilot slot we discussed is held for you until **[3 business days from today]**. If you want to lock it in, just reply "yes" — I'll send the pilot agreement (1 page, no surprises).

Two quick favours if you have a moment:

1. **Which of the 5 features stood out most?** Knowing this helps us prioritise your migration.
2. **Could you intro me to one peer who'd also benefit?** I'm trying to onboard 5 PMs this quarter — a warm intro is worth 10 cold calls.

Looking forward to building this together.

[Your name]
Founder, Manara OS
📱 [your phone] · 📧 [your email]
📅 [your Calendly]
🌐 manaraos.ae

---

**Customisation cheatsheet:**
- Replace `[N]` and `[X]` with their actual numbers from your discovery
- Pick 1 specific moment from the demo where they reacted positively, paraphrase it
- Always attach something (one-pager, recording, whitepaper) — never just text
- Always have a deadline ("held until [date]") — creates gentle pressure

## Template 2 — 3-day nudge (no response)

**Subject line:** `Quick thought on [their specific pain]`

**Pre-header:** `Saw something this morning that reminded me of you`

---

Hi [first name],

No response is response — I get it, you're busy running [their company name].

But I wanted to share something specific. This morning I was helping another UAE PM with [their stated pain — e.g. "vendor reliability"], and I built them a custom report showing **exactly how much they were over-paying per vendor category** versus our marketplace median.

Took 12 minutes. Result was eye-opening.

Want me to do the same for [their company]? You'd just need to send me a CSV export of your last 12 months of vendor invoices (or even just a screenshot of 10 random ones). I'll come back with:

1. A line-by-line over/under analysis
2. The 3 categories where you're bleeding the most
3. A projected year-one savings number — yours to keep whether you sign or not

No commitment. Just useful data.

Same calendar link if a 15-min call is easier: [Calendly]

[Your name]

---

**When to use this:**
- Demo went well but they haven't responded in 3 days
- They said "let me think" without a specific objection
- You suspect they're shopping you against a competitor

**Why this works:**
- Offers concrete value with zero ask
- Uses social proof ("another UAE PM") without naming
- Lowers commitment from "buy" to "send a CSV"

## Template 3 — 1-week nudge (still no response)

**Subject line:** `Closing your file — quick yes or no`

**Pre-header:** `One question`

---

Hi [first name],

I respect your time, so I'll keep this to one question.

After the demo last week, should I:

**A.** Keep your file open and check back in 30 days
**B.** Close your file — wrong timing, wrong fit, or just not interested
**C.** Connect us on a different call to address what's blocking

Whatever the answer, I won't push. Just hate guessing.

[Your name]

---

**When to use this:**
- 7+ days of silence after demo
- 4+ days after the nudge email

**Why this works:**
- A→B→C framing makes "B" easy without guilt → you get honest feedback OR a real reason
- "Closing your file" is a soft pattern-interrupt — they suddenly value the slot
- Two-line emails get 4× higher response than long ones at this stage

**Expected outcomes:**
- 25-30% reply with B (you learn the real objection — gold)
- 30-40% reply with A (you have a real follow-up date)
- 15-25% reply with C (you have the meeting back)
- 15-25% never reply (genuinely uninterested — move on)

## Subject line patterns that get opened in UAE B2B

| Pattern | Why it works | Example |
|---|---|---|
| `[Name], [number]` | Personal + specific | "Khalifa, the 3 numbers" |
| `Quick thought on [pain]` | Curiosity + relevance | "Quick thought on vendor reliability" |
| `Closing your file` | Loss aversion | "Closing your file — quick yes or no" |
| `[Number] of [number] slots` | Scarcity | "2 of 5 pilot slots remaining" |
| `Saw [thing] reminded me of you` | Personal | "Saw something this morning" |

## Patterns that get IGNORED in UAE B2B

| Pattern | Why it fails |
|---|---|
| `Following up on our meeting` | Too generic, looks automated |
| `Just checking in` | No value, lazy |
| `Reaching out about...` | Cold spam pattern |
| Long subject lines (>50 chars) | Truncated on mobile |
| Emojis in subject line | Hits spam filters more often |

## What to send WITH every email

Always include at least one of:

1. **Personalised attachment** — pre-filled ROI calculator for their unit count
2. **Video** — 90-second Loom showing their specific pain area
3. **Article/data** — UAE-specific stat (e.g. "UAE PMs lose 18% owners/yr to trust")
4. **Calendar link** — make it stupid-easy to book

Email-only follow-ups feel cheap. Always provide something to look at.

---

## The 48-hour rule

If you haven't heard back in 48 hours, **call them**. Not WhatsApp. Phone.

Voice on phone > Voice note on WhatsApp > Text WhatsApp > Email.

UAE business is relationship-driven. The deal closes on the phone, not in an inbox.
