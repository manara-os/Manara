# Manara OS — Live Demo Script

**Total time:** 15 minutes (12 product + 3 close)
**Format:** Sitting next to them at a laptop, OR sharing screen on Zoom/Teams
**Login:** Use the seeded demo workspace. Test it before the meeting.

---

## Before the meeting (15 min prep)

1. **Log in** to your local/staging portal. Verify all 9 features render.
2. **Open these tabs in your browser** (in order):
   1. `/dashboard` — PM Admin home with AI Suggestions
   2. `/owners/[seeded-owner-id]` — Khalifa Al Mansoori (has the most rich data)
   3. `/tenants/[seeded-tenant-id]` — Nadia Al Farsi (has WhatsApp thread + AECB)
   4. `/tickets/[seeded-open-ticket-id]` — open ticket with bids
   5. `/compliance` — full compliance calendar
   6. `/vendors` — leaderboard at top
   7. `/reviews` — Reviews & NPS dashboard
   8. `/reports/ai` — AI Intelligence Reports
3. **Pre-load one Arabic toast** — click the language switcher to Arabic, then back to English, so the dictionary loads.
4. **Close email, Slack, anything that might ping.** Notifications during demos kill momentum.
5. **Disable browser autofill** so no PII shows in demo.

---

## 0:00 — Open with their problem (30 seconds)

**Don't** start by demoing.
**Do** ask:

> "Before I show you anything — what's the one thing about your current setup that drives you most crazy? Genuinely curious."

**Listen.** Whatever they say, reframe the demo around that specifically. Common answers:

| If they say... | Lead with... |
|---|---|
| "Compliance dates slip" | Compliance Calendar (skip to 9:00 first) |
| "Owners don't trust me" | Receipt Vault + Tax Certificate (skip to 3:00) |
| "Vendors are unreliable" | Bid Marketplace + Leaderboard (skip to 7:00) |
| "Tenants take forever to pay" | AECB Credit Reporting + AI calling (skip to 5:00) |
| "I spend all day on WhatsApp" | WhatsApp 2-way thread + AI agent |
| "Statements take a week" | Investor Dashboard (already exists) |
| "Everything" | Default order below |

This shows you listened, not that you have a deck.

## 13:00 — The close (2 min)

**Say:**
> "[Their name], I've shown you the platform. You've seen the depth. Here's what I'd love to do next."

**Offer the three options from Slide 5** (deep dive / pilot / co-design).

**SHUT UP.** Let them respond. Silence wins the close.

**If they ask "how much?"**, answer immediately, full pricing, no flinching. Refer them to slide 4.

**If they ask "can I see the mobile app?"**, show the QR codes to install (you'll have these once the apps are on TestFlight).

**If they say "send me more info"** — that's a soft no. Push back: *"Happy to. But on a scale of 1-10, how excited are you about what you just saw?"* If they say <7, ask what would make it a 9. If they say 7+, ask "what's stopping you from pilot signup right now?"

## Common objections + responses

### "We're already happy with [Buildium / AppFolio / ManagePac / Excel]"

> "That's great. Two questions: does your current tool report to AECB? And does it generate FTA tax certificates? [pause for 'no'] Then it's UAE-illiterate by half. We're not asking you to replace what works — we're asking you to add what's missing. 90-day pilot, no card, you keep your current tool."

### "It's too expensive"

> "Compare it to the cost of ONE compliance fine, or ONE owner churning over a maintenance dispute. Year-one ROI is positive on vendor savings alone — the bid marketplace pays for us. If after 90 days you don't see the math, you don't pay."

### "We don't have the time to migrate"

> "We do the migration. You give us an export from your current tool plus 2 hours of one of your admin's time. Five business days, you're live. Time investment from you: less than an afternoon."

### "I need to think about it"

> "Completely fair. What specifically do you need to think about? [listen] Right — here's what I'll do. I'll send you a 5-minute personalised video addressing exactly that. Can I have your number for the follow-up?"

### "Send me a proposal"

> "Will do. While I'm here — what would be in your dream proposal that's not in our pricing page? [listen] Got it. Let me write that into the proposal. Quick: what timeline would you decide on?"

### "Let me discuss with my partner / boss"

> "Smart. When you do, what's the one thing about Manara they'll push back on? [listen] Let me address that now, then your conversation goes smoother. Also — happy to join that conversation. 15 minutes max."

## Demo killers — avoid these

- ❌ Showing the empty `/listings` page or any page with no seed data
- ❌ Apologising for "still in beta" or "not yet built"
- ❌ Reading the screen aloud — they can read
- ❌ Saying "and you can also..." three times in a row — pick the killer feature and stop
- ❌ Showing the API/Swagger docs unless they ask
- ❌ Talking about competitors by name negatively — punch up, not down
- ❌ Promising features that don't exist yet — say "on roadmap, Q3"
- ❌ Demo-ing past 15 minutes — they will tune out at minute 16

---

## Demo confidence drill (do this once before your first real demo)

1. Set a 15-minute timer.
2. Run the entire script alone, narrating out loud.
3. Time-box each section. If you go over, cut content.
4. Record yourself on Zoom and watch it back at 1.5×.
5. Notice every "um," "kind of," and apologetic phrasing. Eliminate them.

By the third run-through you'll be doing it at 11 minutes and have 4 minutes for objections. That's the sweet spot.
